## 可选增益

**Aberration**

· Increases the Agent's 失衡 累积 比率 by 20%.
· When inflicting Attribute 失衡 on enemies, all squad members gain 60 points of 失衡 Proficiency, and 伤害 caused by Disorder increases by 40% for 15 seconds.

**Frosty Thunder**

· The Agent's Electric 伤害 and Ice 伤害 will increase by 25%.
· 伤害 dealt by Basic Attack, Dodge Counter, and Dash Attack increases by 40%, ignoring 15% of the enemy's 防御.

**Rapid Aid**

· The Agent's Energy generation rate increases by 25%, and the 伤害 dealt by Quick Assist increases by 50%.
· Each use of Quick Assist increases all squad members' 攻击 by 10% for 20s, stacking up to 3 times. Repeated triggers reset the duration.
