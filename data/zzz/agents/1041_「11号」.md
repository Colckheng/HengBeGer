### 代理人情报

**Trust Rank "Close Friend"**

Master, based on your firsthand account and Agent Anby's testimony, Soldier 11's real name is Harin. She's one of the clones from New Eridu's former clone soldier project and, like Anby, once served in the Defense Force's Silver Squad.

This research project produced multiple clones programmed to obey the upper ranks of the Defense Force without question. However, due to various complications, it was scrapped years ago. The Defense Force sought to erase the unethical experiment and its results, preserving only one soldier as a symbolic success. Anby had been the intended candidate, but her self-destructive act to protect Soldier 11 led to Soldier 11 being the one preserved. Magus, captain of Obol Squad, later chose to bring Soldier 11 into her squad.

Having never known the truth, Soldier 11 grew up resenting Anby, feeling abandoned and betrayed. She set her sights on becoming stronger than Anby — stronger than anyone. Still, she developed a strong rapport with her squadmates in Obol Squad, and now considers them her most important allies.
