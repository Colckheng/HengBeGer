## 核心被动（Lv.6）

### Core Passive: Stealthy Paws

When Nekomata's Dodge Counter or Quick Assist hits an enemy, her 伤害 increases by 60% for 40s. Repeated triggers reset the duration.
Nekomata gains 40 Purr Energy upon entering the battlefield. In Investigation Zone mode, this effect can trigger once every 180s.
While Nekomata is in combat, she gains 1 Purr Energy every second, up to a maximum of 60. Different skills restore different amounts of Purr Energy when they hit an enemy.
When Nekomata has at least 40 Purr Energy and after triggering a Perfect Dodge, press to consume 30 Purr Energy and activate Dodge: Disappearing Tail. Then press to perform Dodge Counter: Fluffy Piercing Claw once without consuming Purr Energy.
When Nekomata has at least 40 Purr Energy, hold to consume 40 Purr Energy and activate Dodge Counter: Fluffy Piercing Claw.
When using Dodge Counter: Fluffy Piercing Claw, Nekomata enters the Pawpad Ambush state for 40s. Repeated triggers reset the duration. While Pawpad Ambush is active, all of Nekomata's attacks count as hitting from behind.
While Pawpad Ambush is active, Nekomata's attacks trigger Super Mean Pawprint, dealing an additional instance of Physical 伤害 equal to 30% of her 攻击. This effect can trigger once every 1s.
Nekomata restores 20 additional Purr Energy when using her Ultimate, 10 when using her Chain Attack, and 5 when using her EX Special Attack.

### Additional Ability: Catwalk

When another character in your squad is a Support character, or shares the same attribute or Faction:
After Nekomata uses Dodge: Disappearing Tail, or after any squad member inflicts Assault on an enemy, Nekomata's EX Special Attack and Dodge Counter deal 35% more 伤害 on hit. This effect stacks up to 2 times and lasts 30s. Repeated triggers reset the duration.

### 角色设定

Nekomiya Mana. Refers to herself as "Nekomata."
A cat Thiren with feline traits, possessing great dexterity when hunting and an insatiable curiosity about the outside world.
Sometimes mischievous, she engages in harmless pranks. But when she sets her sights on a target, her feline-like agility and focus are enough to leave others in awe.
She's most interested in other people's wallets.
Recommendation: Always keep an eye on your wallet when Nekomata is around.

In the past, Nekomata was a member of the long-standing Red Fang Gang. Its leader, Miguel Silver, took on a fatherly role in her life. Nonetheless, due to differing beliefs from the gang, she opted to part ways and go it alone.
Following certain encounters involving Nicole and others, she eventually opted to enlist in Gentle House, thus becoming the third employee on the team.
